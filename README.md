# Daybreak

An alarm app that actually gets you out of bed. Daybreak rings at full volume even when your phone is on silent or in a Focus mode, wakes you with a song from your own music library, and, if you want, won't stop until you've walked a few steps or solved some math. No more sleeping through a muted alarm or swiping it off half-asleep.

## Features

- **Wakes you through silent mode and Focus.** Unlike a normal notification, Daybreak plays your alarm at full volume even if your ringer is off or a Focus is on.
- **Wake-up missions.** Choose to make the alarm refuse to stop until you've completed a task. It's a lot harder to fall back asleep once you're up and moving. Missions are optional; you can also just tap to dismiss.
  - **Steps:** walk a set number of steps.
  - **Math:** solve a few arithmetic problems.
  - **Shake:** shake your phone hard, a set number of times.
  - **Scan:** scan a QR code or barcode you registered, like the one on your toothpaste, so you have to get up and walk to it.
  - **Photo:** take a photo of a spot you registered, like the bathroom sink.
  - **Memory:** watch a pattern of tiles light up, then repeat it. Each round gets longer.
  - **Typing:** retype a short wake-up phrase, word for word.
- **Wake-up check.** Five minutes after you stop the alarm, Daybreak asks if you're still awake. If you don't answer within 90 seconds, the alarm rings again.
- **Your music as the alarm.** Pick any song from your Apple Music or iTunes library, or choose one of the built-in tones.
- **Quick alarm.** Need a nap? Tap the timer button and stack up buttons like +5 min, +30 min, and +1 hour to set a one-off alarm without picking a clock time.
- **Gentle volume ramp.** The alarm can fade in over a few seconds instead of blasting you awake instantly.
- **Snooze on your terms.** Set how long snooze lasts and how many times it's allowed.
- **Sunrise countdown.** The home screen shows a sunrise arc that fills as your next alarm approaches.
- **Set alarms with Siri.** "Set a Daybreak alarm," "When's my next Daybreak alarm," or turn alarms on and off by voice.
- **Backup system alarm.** As a safety net, Daybreak also sets a system alarm so you'll still be woken even if the app gets closed.

## How to use it

### Setting an alarm

1. Tap the **+** button in the top-right.
2. Spin the dial to your wake-up time.
3. Choose which days it repeats (leave all off for a one-time alarm).
4. Pick your sound — tap **Wake-up song** to choose from your library, or pick a built-in **notification tone**.
5. Choose a **wake-up mission**, or **None**. For **Scan** and **Photo**, you register your code or spot right there: tap **Scan a code** or **Take a photo**.
6. Adjust snooze, vibration, and the **wake-up check** under the options, then tap **Save**.

### Setting a quick alarm

1. Tap the **timer** button in the top-left.
2. Tap the **+** buttons to build up the time — for example, **+1 hour**, **+5 min**, and **+1 min** sets an alarm for 1 hour and 6 minutes from now.
3. The exact ring time is shown as you go. Tap **Set alarm**.

### Turning alarms on and off

Flip the switch on any alarm to enable or disable it without deleting it.

### Deleting an alarm

**Swipe left** on an alarm and tap Delete, or **press and hold** it and choose Delete.

### When the alarm goes off

Your alarm rings and the wake-up screen takes over. If it has a mission, tap **Start mission** and complete it to stop the alarm. Otherwise, tap **Stop**. If snooze is allowed, you'll see a Snooze button with how many snoozes you have left.

Can't find the code you registered, or can't match your photo? After a minute you can solve a few math problems instead, so you're never stuck with a ringing alarm.

If the wake-up check is on, tap **I'm up** (or the "Still awake?" notification) when it asks.

> **Keep Daybreak open in the background.** For the alarm to ring at full volume, don't force-close the app (swiping it away in the app switcher). If you do, Daybreak will warn you that your alarm may not ring reliably. Leaving it running overnight is expected — keep your phone charging.

## Permissions

Daybreak asks for a few permissions the first time you use the features that need them:

- **Notifications** *(required)* — so your alarm can reach you.
- **Motion & Fitness** *(optional)* — to count your steps for the steps mission.
- **Music Library** *(optional)* — to play a song from your library as the alarm.
- **Camera** *(optional)* — for the scan and photo missions.
- **Alarms** *(optional)* — for the backup system alarm.

You can grant or change these any time in the Settings app under Daybreak.
